# 📱 Admin User & CMS Guide — Yuvanthika Aquacare & Solar Care Systems

Welcome to the **Yuvanthika Aquacare & Solar Care Systems Admin Dashboard & CMS**.

This guide is designed so that anyone can log in from a smartphone, tablet, or desktop computer to manage the entire website content, upload project photos, view customer enquiries, publish blog articles, and add new product entries **without touching code, GitHub, or Vercel**.

---

## 🔐 1. How to Log In to the Admin Panel

1. Open your web browser on your phone or computer.
2. Navigate to:  
   👉 **`https://yuvanthikaaquasolar.in/admin/login`**
3. Enter your Admin Credentials:
   - **Default Email**: `admin@yuvanthikaaquasolar.in`
   - **Default Password**: `admin123` (or `Admin@Yuvanthika2026!`)
4. Click **Sign In to Dashboard**.

> 💡 **Tip for Mobile Users**: Tap the browser menu and select **"Add to Home Screen"** to create an app icon for 1-tap access from your smartphone!

---

## 📥 2. Viewing & Exporting Customer Leads

1. Go to **`Leads & Enquiries`** in the left sidebar (or top menu on mobile).
2. You will see a real-time list of all incoming customer requests containing:
   - Customer Name & Location
   - Phone Number
   - Service Requested (e.g. *Industrial RO Plant*, *Water Softener*)
3. **Direct Action Buttons**:
   - Tap **Call** to dial the customer immediately.
   - Tap **WhatsApp** to open a direct WhatsApp chat.
4. **Export CSV**: Click the **Export All Leads CSV** button to download an Excel-compatible spreadsheet of all customer enquiries.

---

## 💼 3. Adding New Project Case Studies

1. Go to **`Projects & Case Studies`** ➔ Click **`+ Create New Case Study`**.
2. Fill in the simple form:
   - **Project Title**: e.g. *50,000 LPH Industrial RO Plant for Textile Dyeing*
   - **Customer Category**: Select *Industrial*, *Commercial*, or *Residential*.
   - **Location & District**: e.g. *Reddipalayam, Karur*.
   - **Problem Faced**: e.g. *Boiler scale build-up due to 3,200 PPM hard water*.
   - **Solution Provided**: e.g. *Custom high-pressure RO unit with anti-scalant dosing*.
   - **Featured Checkbox**: Check *Mark as Featured* to display this project directly on the homepage!
3. Click **Publish Project Case Study**.
4. The system automatically creates a dedicated page at `/projects/your-project-title` with full SEO keywords and Open Graph sharing cards!

---

## 🖼️ 4. Uploading Installation Photos & Project Videos

1. Go to **`Media Library`** or **`Installation Gallery`**.
2. Select the destination folder (e.g. `/Projects`, `/Products`, `/Gallery`).
3. Tap **Choose File** and select photos (JPG, PNG, WebP) or video clips (MP4) from your phone gallery or camera.
4. Tap **Start Upload**.
5. The image is instantly uploaded and ready for inclusion in projects, products, or gallery pages.

---

## 📝 5. Writing & Publishing Blog Articles

1. Go to **`Blog Articles`** ➔ Click **`+ Create New Blog`**.
2. Enter the Article Title (e.g., *How to Select the Right RO Water Purifier in Karur*).
3. Paste or type your article content.
4. Click **Publish Article**.
5. Google SEO metadata, Twitter Cards, and Schema.org structured data are generated automatically!

---

## 🛍️ 6. Managing Products Catalog

1. Go to **`Products Catalog`**.
2. Search for existing products or click **`+ Add New Product`**.
3. Set the product name, price display (e.g., *₹9,500* or *Call for Price*), capacity, and specifications.
4. The product card automatically updates on the homepage, category pages, and search.

---

## ⚙️ 7. Updating Company Phone, Address & Homepage Text

1. Go to **`Site Settings`**.
2. Update any business detail:
   - Display Phone Number
   - WhatsApp Number
   - Official Address
   - Homepage Main Hero Banner Headline
3. Click **Save All Settings**.
4. Changes take effect across the entire website instantly.

---

## 👥 8. Super Admin User Management

*(Super Admin Role Only)*
1. Go to **`Admin Users`**.
2. Click **Create Admin User** to add new store managers or family members with controlled access.
