## 📅 Step-by-Step Posting Workflow

### Step 1: Open Google Business Profile Manager
1. Log in to [Google Business Profile Manager](https://business.google.com/).
2. Select **Yuvanthika Aquacare & Solar Care Systems - Karur**.
3. Click on **Add Update** -> **Add Update / Post**.

### Step 2: Formulate GBP Post
Each post generated by our system follows this high-converting format:

- **Post Title / Headline**: `🎉 New Project Completed: [Project Type] Installation in [District]!`
- **Description**:
  ```text
  Yuvanthika Aquacare & Solar Care Systems successfully installed a [Capacity] [Project Type] in [Location], [District].

  🔹 Problem Solved: [Problem]
  🔹 Solution: [Solution]
  🔹 Results: [Top Benefit / Outcome]

  Looking for reliable water treatment or solar care in [District] or across Tamil Nadu? 
  Call us today at +91 84288 88854 or visit https://yuvanthikaaquasolar.in/projects/[slug]!
  ```
- **Action Button**: Select **Learn More** or **Call Now**.
- **Button URL**: `https://yuvanthikaaquasolar.in/projects/[slug]`

### Step 3: Attach High-Quality Photos
1. Upload 1 to 3 clear, well-lit photos of the installed RO plant, water softener, solar array, ETP/STP, or technician team.
2. Ensure image filenames contain keywords (e.g., `industrial-ro-plant-karur.jpg`).
